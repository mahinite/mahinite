### `01` &nbsp; THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

</div>

I'm Mahin — a student interested in the space where software, AI, and thoughtful product design meet.

I like taking ideas from **"this would be useful"** to something people can actually use. That means exploring a problem, figuring out what the simplest useful version looks like, and then working through the details until it feels right.

Most projects start with a question:

> **"What would a useful version of this look like?"**

Then I try to build it.

### `04` &nbsp; HOW I LIKE TO BUILD

*Start with the problem. Keep the solution intentional.*

</div>

I prefer practical problems over technology for its own sake. I like clean interfaces, useful details, and products that don't make simple things harder than they need to be.

```text
  💡  NOTICE A PROBLEM
   │
   ▼
  🧭  UNDERSTAND THE FRICTION
   │
   ▼
  🔨  BUILD THE SMALLEST USEFUL VERSION
   │
   ▼
  🧪  TEST · BREAK · REFINE
   │
   ▼
  🚀  SHIP, LEARN, REPEAT
   │
   └──────────────► "What could be better?"
```

AI tools are part of my workflow, but curiosity and understanding stay at the centre. I want to get better at making good decisions, not just generating more code.
